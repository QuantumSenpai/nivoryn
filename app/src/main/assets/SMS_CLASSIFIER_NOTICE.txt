SMS classifier training source: Almeida, T. & Hidalgo, J. (2011), SMS Spam Collection, UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CC84 . UCI states CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Adaptation: Nivoryn multinomial Naive Bayes weights, NFKC/lowercase bounded unigram features, deduplicated stratified 80/20 split (seed 228), threshold 0.99. Dataset messages are not bundled, only learned coefficients. English historical spam labels do not establish scam/fraud or multilingual performance. This component does not replace Llama/ExecuTorch reasoning.
