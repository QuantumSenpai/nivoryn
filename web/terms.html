Release status

This page offers a signed development/test APK, not a production release. Its local heuristic checks are incomplete and can make mistakes. Llama reasoning runs locally after verified model import. The English spam proxy, PCAP replay and static APK indicators have limited evaluated scope; live VPN monitoring, general malware-behaviour monitoring and transcription are unavailable.
